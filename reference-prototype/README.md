## Proposed revenue model

Neeo Plus is a recurring subscription for advanced preference filters and saved presets. Matching, responding to likes, chat, safety and privacy stay free. US$9.99 monthly is illustrative, not a researched recommendation or approved price. Do not treat the sample renewal date as live billing information. Net revenue depends on subscribers, collected local prices, tax, store fees, refunds and operating costs; no earnings forecast is promised.

The membership screens cover comparison, review, native-store handoff, success, cancellation, pending, failure, restoration, missing purchases, active membership, renewal cancellation, billing issues and expiry. Native checkout, entitlement reconciliation and actual store management must be implemented and verified separately.
