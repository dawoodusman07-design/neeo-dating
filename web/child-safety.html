Neeo has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

  • Neeo is strictly 18+. We age-gate at signup and remove any account we believe belongs to a minor.
  • Users can report a profile as “May be under 18” from any profile or chat; these reports are prioritised at the top of our review queue.
  • We remove CSAM immediately, preserve evidence as required, and report to NCMEC and relevant authorities.
  • We comply with applicable child-safety laws in every market we operate.
