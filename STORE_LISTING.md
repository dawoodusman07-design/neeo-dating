## Description

Something real starts with hello.

Neeo is a dating app for people who want something real. Every day you get a small set of thoughtful introductions — not an endless feed — so you can actually get to know someone.

**Intentional by design**
• A few introductions a day, chosen with care
• Everyone shows what they're looking for: long-term, life partner, or figuring it out
• Like a specific photo or prompt, and add a comment to start a real conversation
• Voice prompts so you can hear someone before you meet

**Privacy that's free, always**
• Blur your photos until you match or choose to share
• Screenshot protection
• Choose who can find you
• Hide your profile from people in your phone's contacts

**Safety first**
• Selfie verification and Verified badges
• Report and block from any profile or chat
• Gentle "Are you sure?" prompts before hurtful messages, and auto-blur for messages that might upset you
• Date check-in: share your plans with a trusted contact
• Optional chaperone (wali) mode

**At your own pace**
• No reply timers, no streaks, no pressure
• Plan a first date right in the chat

Neeo Plus adds more daily introductions, see who liked you, advanced preferences and undo. Privacy and safety tools are always free.

Neeo is for adults 18 and over.
